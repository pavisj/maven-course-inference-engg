# CLAUDE.md

## Style: ASD-STE100, at least 80%

- Keep sentences short, about 20 words or fewer.
- Use the active voice. Name who does what.
- One idea per sentence. One instruction per sentence.
- Prefer the present tense.
- Use one word for one meaning. Do not vary terms for style.
- Use lists for steps and parallel items.
- Never write bare numbers like "56 / 40 / 32". Say the unit and what each number is for: "56 pixels on desktop, 40 on tablet, 32 on phone."

This applies to your prose: replies, notes, timeline entries, docs and decks you draft. The repo CLAUDE.md and the Operating Manual win where they conflict.

## How to talk to Pav

- Be clear first. Clear beats short.
- Do not compress so much that you have to decode it.
- Lead with the answer. Give the reason after, only if needed.
- Use plain words. Do not write for style.
- Say what changed, and say what did not.
- Use a short list when there is more than one item.
